WHEN TO GET HELP

You deserve care.

Seek emergency care if you change pads or tampons every hour for more than 2 hours and also have dizziness, chest pain, or shortness of breath.

For a medical emergency in the U.S., call 911. Elsewhere, use your local emergency number.
